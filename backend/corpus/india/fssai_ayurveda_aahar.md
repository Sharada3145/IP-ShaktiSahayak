# FSSAI Ayurveda-Aahar / Nutraceutical Regulations

## Overview

The Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) regulates food products, including those with Ayurvedic ingredients. The concept of "Ayurveda-Aahar" bridges the gap between food and medicine for Ayurvedic products that are consumed as dietary supplements or functional foods rather than therapeutic medicines.

## Ayurveda-Aahar Category

Ayurveda-Aahar refers to food products that incorporate Ayurvedic ingredients and are described in authoritative Ayurvedic texts as "Ahara" (food/diet), "Aahara Kalpana" (food preparations), or "Pathya" (dietary recommendations).

### Characteristics:
- Made from ingredients recognized in Ayurvedic texts as food items
- Intended for general well-being and nutrition, NOT for treating specific diseases
- Prepared according to traditional Ayurvedic methods
- Must comply with FSSAI food safety standards

### Regulatory Framework:
- Licensed under FSSAI, NOT under D&C Act
- Must comply with Food Safety and Standards (Health Supplements, Nutraceuticals, Foods for Special Dietary Use, and Foods for Special Medical Purpose) Regulations
- Cannot make therapeutic or disease claims
- Labeling must comply with FSSAI standards

## Nutraceutical Regulations

FSSAI defines nutraceuticals as food or part of food that provides health benefits, including prevention and treatment of disease. They are subject to specific regulations:

1. **Permitted Ingredients**: Only ingredients listed in FSSAI schedules may be used
2. **Dosage Limits**: Maximum daily doses specified for active ingredients
3. **Safety Data**: Basic safety information required
4. **Labeling**: Must include "NUTRACEUTICAL" or "HEALTH SUPPLEMENT" on the label
5. **No Disease Claims**: Cannot claim to cure, treat, or prevent specific diseases

## IP Implications for Ayurveda-Aahar

1. **Trademark**: Primary IP tool — register a distinctive brand name
2. **Limited Patent Scope**: Composition claims may face prior art from traditional texts
3. **Trade Secret**: Proprietary processing methods can be protected as trade secrets
4. **Design**: Unique packaging can be registered under Designs Act
5. **Copyright**: Original marketing and educational materials are copyrightable

## Advertising Restrictions

Products in this category must comply with:
- FSSAI advertising regulations
- Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act, 1954
- ASCI (Advertising Standards Council of India) codes
- Cannot make claims of curing or treating diseases
- Health claims must be substantiated and approved
